# Pitch It 
## A product jointly ideated by Kriyadocs and Cadmore
Pitch It is a product aimed at helping an author present their best foot forward and for other stakeholders to provide structured, helpful feedback in return. The product can be integrated with all major peer review platforms. 
### Why
The main reason to create this product is to help the journal improve the researcher experience through submission and peer review and upon acceptance improve the discoverability of the article. The key challenges publishers face today are

1. AI submissions and paper mills

2. Huge volume of submissions

3. Cumbersome screening processes

4. Poor reviewer acceptance rates

5. Inadequate metadata and content for AEO and GEO
### Key Outcomes
1. Establish the identity of the author and act like a KYC

2. Help the editor decide if the manuscript has merit and is worthy of review

3. Help the reviewer decide if the manuscript is interesting to be reviewed and accept to review.

4. Allow the reviewer and editor share constructive feedback with the author that will help the author address the key issues.

5. For articles that are accepted, the author can produce video abstracts and lay summaries to serve as a valuable addition to the article and enhance discovery.
### Capabilities
1. At Submission

   1. Authors will launch the interface from their peer review platform.

   2. Authors  can submit their personalized pitch along with their article to explain why they should be considered for review and why their research is meaningful.

      1. They will be able to choose from a suitable list of templates and can record their video and attach a slide deck along with it.

      2. The video can be previewed for approval before submitting.

      3. The author will also be presented an anonymized video as well that will anonymize their appearance as well as their voice and can be used for double anonymized peer review workflows.

      4. Once the submission is done, the video will be uploaded to the Cadmore platform and will be captioned and optimized for streaming. The video link will be shared with the peer review platform.

2. At Peer Review

   3. Editors can view the embedded video or launch the interface and can use it as part of their evaluation. If they so decide, they can make the video available for reviewers as well. 

   4. If the journal is double anonymized, only the anonymized video will be shared.

   5. Reviewers and editors will have the option to share video feedback in return and an anonymized version will be created for any single or double anonymized workflows. 

3. At Acceptance

   6. Editors can launch the interface to record a congratulatory video on their submission creating greater connect with the journal.

   7. Authors can create 2 more videos at this time namely Video abstracts and Lay summaries that will help their audience understand their work and improve discoverability.

   8. They can also create a more elaborate video article for journals that support this article type.

4. At publishing

   9. The article will be published to the hosting platform with the video hosted on Cadmore.

   10. Viewers will be able to watch the video and share comments with the author.

### Expected benefits
1. High author satisfaction rate (irrespective of the decision)

2. Charged up editors

3. Engaged reviewers

4. Connected audiences

5. Improved research outcomes
