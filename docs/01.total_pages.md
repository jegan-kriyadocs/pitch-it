# Pitch It - Complete Application Pages Specification & Prompt

> **Project Concept**: Jointly ideated by **Kriyadocs** and **Cadmore Media**.  
> **Mission**: Modernize academic peer review through short video pitches, AI-ready KYC author verification, double-blind anonymization, and post-acceptance video abstracts / lay summaries.

---

## 1. System Architecture & Page Overview (6 Pages)

To cleanly separate user roles (Author, Editor, Reviewer, and Public Reader) and provide a friction-free demo and production workflow, the platform comprises **6 dedicated pages**:

| # | Route / URL | Page Title | Primary Actor / Persona | Stage |
|---|-------------|------------|-------------------------|-------|
| 1 | `/` or `/author-studio` | **Author Submission Studio** | Author / Submitting Researcher | Submission |
| 2 | `/editor-triage` | **Editor Triage Dashboard** | Journal Editor | Screening & Triage |
| 3 | `/reviewer-feedback` | **Reviewer Evaluation Studio** | Peer Reviewer | Peer Review |
| 4 | `/editor-congratulations` | **Editor Congratulatory Studio** | Journal Editor | Acceptance |
| 5 | `/author-acceptance` | **Author Post-Acceptance Studio** | Accepted Author | Post-Acceptance Assets |
| 6 | `/published-preview` | **Published Article Showcase** | Readers, Researchers, Media | Publishing / Discovery |

---

## 2. Detailed Page-by-Page Specifications

### Page 1: Author Submission Studio
* **Route**: `/` or `/author-studio`
* **Actor**: Author (Researcher, Physician, Academic)
* **Core Purpose**: Allows the author to create and submit a concise (2-3 minute) video pitch with slides to argue why their manuscript has novelty and deserves peer review.
* **Key Features**:
  1. **Template Selection**: Choose pitch archetype (`Clinical Trial`, `Basic Science Discovery`, `Methodology & Review`).
  2. **Slide Deck Integration**: Slide-by-slide navigator with cue cards / presenter notes.
  3. **Live Camera & Audio Recording**: WebRTC video/audio capture with countdown, live timer, and re-record controls.
  4. **Double-Blind Anonymization Engine**:
     - Toggle visual face-blurring / avatar mask.
     - Toggle pitch-shifted audio modulation (for double-blind journals).
  5. **Cadmore Media Upload Pipeline**: Auto-generation of video captions, streaming optimization, and submission link to the manuscript tracking system.

---

### Page 2: Editor Triage Dashboard
* **Route**: `/editor-triage`
* **Actor**: Journal Editor / Associate Editor
* **Core Purpose**: Rapid manuscript screening. Instead of reading a 40-page PDF immediately, the editor watches the author's 2-minute video pitch to evaluate merit and verify author identity (KYC).
* **Key Features**:
  1. **Triage Queue**: Split-screen listing pending manuscript submissions with risk and novelty scores.
  2. **Embedded Pitch Player**: Video playback synchronized with the author's pitch slides.
  3. **Author Identity / KYC Badge**: Verification status to combat AI-generated paper mill submissions.
  4. **Triage Decision Suite**:
     - `Accept for Peer Review` (routes to Reviewer invitation).
     - `Request Re-pitch` (prompts author for revised clinical/statistical proof).
     - `Desk Reject` (with rapid structured rationale).
  5. **Reviewer Visibility Controls**: Option to enable full video or strictly anonymized video for reviewers.

---

### Page 3: Reviewer Evaluation Studio
* **Route**: `/reviewer-feedback`
* **Actor**: External Peer Reviewer
* **Core Purpose**: Incentivize busy reviewers by letting them review the pitch video first, then provide structured video/audio feedback alongside traditional critique notes.
* **Key Features**:
  1. **Invitation & Pitch Brief**: Anonymized video player displaying author slides and key statistical proof.
  2. **Double-Blind Reviewer Masking**: Reviewer's own identity protected via pseudonym (`Reviewer 2 - Biostatistician`).
  3. **Video / Audio Feedback Recorder**: Reviewer can record a 90-second conversational commentary instead of drafting a lengthy essay.
  4. **Structured Evaluation Criteria**: Quick score sliders (Methodological Rigor, Originality, Clinical Utility).
  5. **Cadmore Anonymized Audio-Video Pipeline**: Applies voice filter to reviewer's remarks before transmitting to the author.

---

### Page 4: Editor Congratulatory Studio
* **Route**: `/editor-congratulations`
* **Actor**: Journal Editor
* **Core Purpose**: Once peer review is positive and the manuscript is formally accepted, the editor records a personalized congratulatory video to welcome the author to the journal family.
* **Key Features**:
  1. **Accepted Manuscript Brief**: Manuscript title, authors, DOI preview, and final reviewer scores.
  2. **Editor Video Recorder**: Friendly teleprompter prompt template for the editor to record a 60-second welcome/congratulatory greeting.
  3. **Editorial Acceptance Notes**: Text box for publication schedule, embargo dates, and journal issue assignment.
  4. **Transmit to Author Portal**: Sends notification to the author's acceptance dashboard.

---

### Page 5: Author Post-Acceptance Studio
* **Route**: `/author-acceptance`
* **Actor**: Accepted Author
* **Core Purpose**: Post-acceptance media preparation to maximize research reach, citations, and discoverability (AEO/GEO).
* **Key Features**:
  1. **Editor Congratulation Playback**: Author watches the personalized welcome video from the journal editor.
  2. **Dual Video Asset Creator**:
     - **Video Abstract** (3 to 4 minutes): Deep dive into technical methodology and charts for academic peers.
     - **Lay Summary** (90 seconds): Plain-language, high-impact overview for the general public, patients, journalists, and funding agencies.
  3. **Teleprompter & Script Assistant**: AI-assisted bullet point suggestions to turn complex medical/technical jargon into accessible speech.
  4. **Publish to Cadmore Syndication**: Encodes multi-bitrate streams, adds multilingual captions, and generates citation snippets.

---

### Page 6: Published Article Showcase
* **Route**: `/published-preview`
* **Actor**: Readers, Clinicians, Researchers, General Public
* **Core Purpose**: The public-facing journal article hosting page enhanced with Cadmore-powered video tabs, maximizing engagement and generative engine optimization.
* **Key Features**:
  1. **Journal Article Header**: Metadata (Journal Volume, DOI, Open Access badge, Author affiliations).
  2. **Cadmore Universal Media Player**:
     - Tab 1: `Video Abstract` (Technical deep-dive).
     - Tab 2: `Lay Summary` (Plain-language video).
     - Tab 3: `Original Pitch` (Historical submission pitch).
  3. **Interactive Audience Engagement**: Timestamped viewer comments, author replies, citation export, and social sharing.
  4. **Article Full-Text & Figures**: Traditional manuscript view integrated seamlessly below the video player.
