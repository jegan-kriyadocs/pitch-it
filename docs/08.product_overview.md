# Pitch It — Product Overview

A product jointly built by Kriyadocs and Cadmore Media.

This document describes the complete product before the PRD is written. Items marked **[To confirm]** still need validation.

---

## 1. What Pitch It is

Pitch It lets an author send a short video pitch (2–3 minutes) along with their manuscript.

- **Editors** watch the pitch to decide faster whether the manuscript should go to review.
- **Reviewers** watch the pitch before deciding whether to accept a review invitation.
- **Editors and reviewers** can send video, audio or written feedback back to the author.

Pitch It works inside the peer review platforms journals already use: the Kriyadocs portal, Editorial Manager and ScholarOne.

---

## 2. Problems it solves

| Problem publishers face | How Pitch It helps |
|---|---|
| AI-generated submissions and paper mills | Checks the author's identity: ORCID, institutional email and a live recording |
| Too many submissions | A short pitch helps editors make the first decision faster |
| Slow screening | Editors understand the core idea before reading the full manuscript |
| Few reviewers accept invitations | Reviewers see what the research is about before deciding |
| Articles hard to find in AI search engines | Captions, transcripts, and later video abstracts and lay summaries (R2) |

| Release | What is included |

## 4. Who uses it, and where they come from

| User | Where they come from | How they sign in |
|---|---|---|
| **Author** | Starts from the journal's submission form on the peer review platform | ORCID or verified email |
| **Editor** | Already set up by the journal (editorial board, associate editors) | Peer review platform login |
| **Editor-in-Chief** | Already set up by the journal | Peer review platform login. The only person who can see the original (not anonymized) video |
| **Reviewer (existing)** | The journal's reviewer list, or suggested by the author | Secure link in the invitation email, or their reviewer dashboard |
| **Reviewer (new)** | The editor adds them by name and email. The system creates a temporary guest account | Secure link in the invitation email. No password needed |
| **Publisher admin** | Publisher staff | Peer review platform admin login |

**Co-authors:** only one person records the pitch in R1 (the corresponding author, or a co-author chosen to submit). Other co-authors can watch the pitch on the peer review platform before it is submitted.

---

## 5. Where the data comes from, and why

| Data | Comes from | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Manuscript ID, journal, article type | The peer review platform, sent securely when Pitch It opens | The pitch belongs to one manuscript, and the platform already holds that record |
| Author identity | ORCID sign-in and institutional email domain | ORCID proves the author's publication history; the email domain proves where they work |
| Journal rules (review type, required or optional, limits) | Journal settings set by the publisher admin | Rules differ for each journal and article type |
| Editor and reviewer accounts | The peer review platform | The platform already manages these people |
| Videos, captions, transcripts | Cadmore Media | Cadmore stores and streams every video |
| Slides | Uploaded by the author (PDF or PPTX) | They support what the author says in the video |

### Who owns users and emails

| Platform | Who manages users and sends emails |
|---|---|
| **Kriyadocs portal** | Kriyadocs. It creates accounts and sends the emails automatically when the manuscript moves forward |
| **Editorial Manager / ScholarOne** | That platform. Pitch It only gives them the video player code, the video link and the reviewer's secure link to add to their own emails |
| **Pitch It sold on its own** | Pitch It keeps its own small store of accounts and secure links **[To confirm / Details in TDD]** |

### Emails (Kriyadocs portal)

1. **To the editor**, when the author submits: "New submission awaiting first review: [Title] — watch the short video pitch."
2. **To the reviewer**, when invited: "Invitation to review for [Journal] — includes a short video pitch from the author."
3. **To the author**, when a decision is made: a link to the feedback page.
4. **Reminders** for reviewers who have not replied or are late. How often reminders are sent: **[To confirm / Set by each publisher]**.

The author also sees the main status on the peer review platform: **Under first review**, **Under review**, **Decision made**.

## 6. Pages

Pitch It has **4 new pages** and **1 new settings tab**. Each user works with at most 2 pages. Smaller steps open as popup windows or side panels on the same page, so users never jump between many pages.

| # | Name | Type | Used by |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pitch It settings | **Tab** inside the existing journal settings page | Publisher admin |
| 2 | Author Studio | **Page** | Author |
| 3 | Editor Dashboard | **Page** | Editor, Editor-in-Chief |
| 4 | Reviewer Page | **Page** | Reviewer |
| 5 | Author Feedback | **Page** | Author |

### 6.1 Pitch It settings (tab, publisher admin)

A new tab inside the journal settings the publisher already uses. Not a new page.

- **Review type:** open, single-blind or double-blind
- **Pitch required or optional:** set for each article type
- **Time limits:** author pitch up to 3 minutes, reviewer feedback up to 90 seconds, editor note up to 60 seconds
- **Video quality and size:** up to 1080p, up to 150 MB
- **Reviewer link expiry:** 14 days by default
- **Reviewer scoring criteria:** the 4 default criteria (see 6.4), editable
- **Reminder emails:** how often and when **[To confirm]**
- **Peer review platform:** Kriyadocs portal, Editorial Manager or ScholarOne

### 6.2 Author Studio (page)

Opens from the "Record Pitch" button on the journal's submission form. Everything the author needs happens on this one page.

**Step 1 — Sign in popup**
- Sign in with ORCID
- Institutional email check
- Consent: the author agrees to being recorded, to the identity check and to how the video is stored (GDPR)

**Step 2 — Choose how to pitch**

Two tabs at the top:

| Tab | What the author does |
|---|---|
| **Record** | Records live in the browser with camera and microphone |
| **Upload** | Uploads a video already made in PowerPoint, Zoom, OBS or similar (.mp4 or .webm) |

Other ways to pitch, for authors who cannot or prefer not to use video:
- **Audio + slides:** voice only, no camera
- **Slides + written script:** the author types a script and the system reads it aloud (text-to-speech). For authors with speech or hearing disabilities, or slow internet

**Step 3 — Prepare and record**
- **Pitch templates:** suggested structures (for example problem, method, results, why it matters)
- **On-screen script (teleprompter):** the author can read while recording
- **Slides:** upload a PDF or PPTX, shown as 5–8 slides synced with the video
- **Timer:** shows the time left (maximum 3 minutes)

**Step 4 — Double-blind settings** (shown only for double-blind journals)
- **Preview switch:** shows the author live how they will look and sound to reviewers
- **Avatar and voice:** double-blind journals allow only a neutral avatar and a neutral voice (no gender or age signals)
- **Warnings while recording:** do not mention your institution, lab name, grant numbers or past papers
- **Automatic check:** scans slides and uploaded videos for names, emails, logos and watermarks
- **Final reminder:** a confirmation popup before submitting

For single-blind and open review journals, the author records with their real face and voice. Avatars and voice styles are optional.

**Step 5 — Preview and submit popup**
- Watch the recording
- Record again, or approve
- Submit. A success message confirms it and shows that the video is being processed

### 6.3 Editor Dashboard (page)

One page split into three areas.

**Left side — Manuscript list**
- All manuscripts waiting for a first decision
- Search and filters: status, article type, identity badge, date
- Shows "Processing" while the video is being prepared

**Right side — Manuscript details**
- Video player with slides, captions and transcript
- **Identity badge:**
    - **Green:** "Identity verified • Live recording confirmed"
    - **Amber:** "Identity verified • Academic ID only (no live recording)" — for uploaded videos and the slides + script option
- **Original video:** only the Editor-in-Chief can view it

**Bottom bar — Decision**
- Three buttons: **Accept for Review**, **Request Re-pitch**, **Desk Reject**
- **Note to the author** (optional): text, or a 30–60 second video note, for Request Re-pitch or Desk Reject. Text templates are available for quick replies
- **Share pitch with reviewers:** on or off. Double-blind journals share only the anonymized video

**Invite reviewers — side panel** (opens on the same page)
- Search the journal's reviewer list
- See reviewers suggested by the author
- Add a new reviewer by name and email (a temporary guest account is created)
- Send the invitation. The reviewer gets a secure link that works for 14 days

### 6.4 Reviewer Page (page)

The reviewer opens this page from the secure link in the invitation email. No password needed.

**Before accepting**
- Watch the author's pitch (anonymized for double-blind journals)
- **Accept** or **Decline** the invitation

**After accepting**
- **Scores** (1 to 5 for each):
    1. **Method:** study design, controls, sample size
    2. **Originality:** what is new compared to existing research
    3. **Statistics:** right analysis, clearly reported data
    4. **Impact:** effect on practice, policy or future research

    Publishers can change these in the settings tab.
- **Feedback** (choose one):

| Option | When | Identity |
|---|---|---|
| **Recorded feedback (default)** | Most journals | Neutral avatar and voice, so the author cannot recognize the reviewer |
| **Recorded feedback with real identity** | Open review journals only | Real face and voice |
| **Upload, audio only or written** | When the reviewer's camera is blocked (for example a hospital firewall) | Anonymized where required |

- Recorded feedback: up to 90 seconds
- **Submit review**
- After submitting: optionally set a password or link ORCID for future reviews

### 6.5 Author Feedback (page)

The author opens this page from the decision email or from the peer review platform. Read-only.

- Editor's decision and note (text or video)
- Each reviewer's feedback: video or audio (anonymized), written comments, scores
- **Re-pitch button:** shown only if the editor asked for a new pitch. Opens the Author Studio again

## 7. Full flow

```
Publisher admin   Sets up the Pitch It settings tab
       │
Author            Submission form → "Record Pitch" → Author Studio
                  Sign in (ORCID) → consent → record or upload → preview → submit
       │
Cadmore           Stores the video, makes the anonymized version, adds captions
       │
Editor            Editor Dashboard → watches pitch, checks identity badge
                  → Accept for Review / Request Re-pitch / Desk Reject
       │                    │                  │
       │                    │                  └─ Author Feedback page
       │                    └─ Author Feedback page → Re-pitch → Author Studio
       ▼
Editor            Invite reviewers (side panel) → email with secure link
       │
Reviewer          Reviewer Page → watches pitch → Accept or Decline
                  → scores + feedback → submit
       │
Author            Author Feedback page → watches feedback, reads scores
```

## 8. What happens to the video after submit

This happens in the background. There is no page for it.

1. The browser uploads the original recording securely to Cadmore.
2. **Cadmore creates the anonymized version on its servers** (avatar and a voice change that cannot be reversed). The live preview in the browser is only a preview; the real anonymized video is always made on the server, so nobody can bypass it. **[To confirm with Cadmore]**
3. Cadmore converts the video for streaming and creates captions and a transcript.
4. Pitch It sends the peer review platform: the video link, the anonymized video link, the captions and transcript, and the identity badge.

**Two versions of every pitch:**

| Version | Who can see it |
|---|---|
| **Original** (real face and voice) | Editor-in-Chief only. Stored encrypted |
| **Anonymized** (double-blind journals) | Editors and reviewers. The only version reviewers ever receive |

Reviewer and editor feedback videos go through the same steps, so authors cannot recognize reviewers.

## 9. Review types and anonymization

| Review type | Author's pitch seen by reviewers as | Reviewer's feedback seen by the author as |
|---|---|---|
| **Open** | Real face and voice | Real face and voice, or anonymized (reviewer's choice) |
| **Single-blind** | Real face and voice | Anonymized |
| **Double-blind** | Neutral avatar and neutral voice only | Anonymized |

## 10. Identity check (KYC)

KYC means "know your customer": checking that the author is a real, verified researcher.

**R1**
- Author signs in with ORCID
- Institutional email domain is checked against the author's affiliation
- Live recording shows that a real person is presenting

**R2**
- AI check for fake (deepfake) videos
- Cross-check with Presubmit integrity data (known paper mills, retracted papers)

---

## 11. Privacy and data storage

- **Consent:** authors agree before recording (GDPR)
- **Encryption:** original videos are stored encrypted
- **Access:** reviewers can never see the original video
- **Rejected manuscripts:** the video file is deleted after 90 days. A digital fingerprint of the video (SHA-256 hash), the author's ORCID, email domain and identity result are kept permanently for integrity investigations
- **Withdrawn manuscripts:** the author can ask for everything to be deleted
- **Accepted manuscripts:** the video is kept, or turned into a video abstract in R2 with the author's approval

## 13. Connecting to peer review platforms

**Order of rollout**
1. **Kriyadocs portal:** first, for the pilot. Kriyadocs controls the whole system, so there are no outside dependencies
2. **Editorial Manager:** one of the two largest peer review platforms
3. **ScholarOne:** the other large platform

**How it connects**
- The platform opens Pitch It with a secure link carrying the manuscript ID, journal and author
- Pitch It sends back the video links, captions, transcript and identity badge
- Editors see the video player inside the platform
- **Kriyadocs 2.0:** Pitch It adds a workflow step for the first review and stores video details with the article. Details in the TDD **[To confirm]**
- **Manuscript transfers:** video links can travel with the manuscript between platforms using the industry MECA package format **[To confirm]**
